# Finance & Resource Log

> Status: 🚧 In progress

This is the public record of money and in-kind resources that flow through RISC-V Ottawa. It complements [§5.5](05-structure-and-governance.md#55-financing-the-layered-model) (where funding comes from) and [§5.9](05-structure-and-governance.md#-59-financial-controls--accounting) (how we handle it responsibly). Amounts are in CAD unless noted.

Until RVO incorporates and opens a bank account ([§5.1](05-structure-and-governance.md#51-legal-structure-decision)), any cash that flows is held by a named organizer on a pass-through basis and recorded here. This file is the interim ledger; once a Treasurer is seated and a bookkeeping tool is chosen ([§5.9](05-structure-and-governance.md#-59-financial-controls--accounting)), it becomes the public-facing summary of those books.

## 7.1 Log

One line per entry, newest at the bottom. Fields, separated by `|`:

`date | type | who | what | amount CAD`

> `type` is `in` (money received), `out` (money spent), or `in-kind` (donated or loaned goods/services; use `-` for amount and note terms in `what`).

## 7.2 Period summaries

Quarterly and annual rollups are published per [§5.8](05-structure-and-governance.md#58-transparency). Until the formal financial-report cadence starts ([§5.9](05-structure-and-governance.md#-59-financial-controls--accounting)), a short note appended here each quarter is enough: total in, total out, ending balance, and any in-kind resources still in use (see [§7.1](#71-log)).
